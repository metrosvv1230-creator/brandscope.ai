How it works

Reads the site

The page and its stylesheets, the same files your browser loads, plus a screenshot of the homepage.

Finds the brand

Primary and secondary logos, the colours the site really uses on buttons and accents, the heading and body fonts, and its corner style.

Sets the board

Everything lands on a 20-tile grid you can edit, switch to dark, and download as a 4K PNG or a PDF with full brand notes.

Some sites block automated visits or build everything with scripts, so a few boards may come back with fewer details. Logos and fonts belong to their brands; boards are for reference, pitches and moodboards.
